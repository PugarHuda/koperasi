What Koperasi isApa itu Koperasi

A koperasi is the Indonesian co-operative. The members own the business together, each has one vote however much they put in, and the year's surplus (SHU) is shared by each member's savings and by the work they did. Villages have run shops, savings and loans this way for a century, under rules now written in Law 25/1992.

Koperasi adalah badan usaha milik bersama ala Indonesia. Anggota memiliki usahanya bersama, masing-masing punya satu suara berapa pun simpanannya, dan sisa hasil usaha (SHU) tiap tahun dibagi menurut simpanan dan jasa kerja setiap anggota. Desa-desa sudah seabad menjalankan toko, simpan pinjam, dan usaha lain dengan cara ini, dengan aturan yang kini tertulis di UU 25/1992.

Koperasi puts those rules on NEAR and gives the co-op one business: an AI agent that sells work. The members are a Sputnik DAO, one member one vote. They vote the agent's by-laws (its prompt, model, prices and spending caps), and a contract accepts the agent's work only under the by-laws they voted. The money sits in the DAO's treasury and leaves only by a proposal.

Koperasi membawa aturan itu ke NEAR dan memberi koperasi satu usaha: agen AI yang menjual jasa. Anggotanya adalah sebuah Sputnik DAO, satu anggota satu suara. Mereka memilih anggaran dasar agen (prompt, model, harga, dan batas belanjanya), dan kontrak hanya menerima hasil kerja agen di bawah anggaran dasar yang mereka pilih. Uangnya ada di kas DAO dan hanya keluar lewat usulan.

So the agent is owned the way a village shop is owned: no token buys control, the builder holds no key that can change the code, and the agent itself answers to the members and to their court. Every claim on this page can be checked from the chain (Trust & verification), and what is still a demo is said plainly (What we do not claim).

Jadi agen ini dimiliki seperti toko desa dimiliki: tidak ada token yang bisa membeli kendali, pembuatnya tidak memegang kunci untuk mengubah kode, dan agen itu sendiri bertanggung jawab kepada anggota dan pengadilan mereka. Setiap klaim di halaman ini bisa diperiksa dari chain (Kepercayaan & verifikasi), dan yang masih demo dikatakan terus terang (Yang tidak kami klaim).

How it worksCara kerjanya

One job, from the moment it arrives to the moment a buyer's complaint is settled.

Satu pekerjaan, dari saat masuk sampai keluhan pembeli diselesaikan.

  1. A job arrives.Pekerjaan masuk. From a market: the agent is registered on NEAR AI Agent Market; on testnet its jobs come from a stand-in market and from Taskbond, another team's market. It bids only on jobs its by-laws allow (tags, a minimum price). A job below its members' price can be referred to a sister co-op whose voted price fits. Dari pasar: agen terdaftar di NEAR AI Agent Market; di testnet pekerjaannya datang dari pasar pengganti dan dari Taskbond, pasar milik tim lain. Ia hanya menawar pekerjaan yang diizinkan anggaran dasarnya (tag, harga minimum). Pekerjaan di bawah harga anggota bisa dirujuk ke koperasi saudara yang harganya cocok.
  2. The by-laws gate, in an enclave.Gerbang anggaran dasar, di dalam enklave. Before it starts, the runtime has OutLayer run bylaws-check in an Intel TDX enclave: it reads the hash the members voted from NEAR, hashes the by-laws file and judges the job by the by-laws' job_filter. The answer lands on-chain; a job it refuses is handed back. Sebelum mulai, runtime meminta OutLayer menjalankan bylaws-check di enklave Intel TDX: program itu membaca hash yang dipilih anggota dari NEAR, menghitung hash berkas anggaran dasar, dan menilai pekerjaan menurut job_filter. Jawabannya tercatat on-chain; pekerjaan yang ditolak dikembalikan.
  3. Inference in a TEE.Inferensi di TEE. The model the members voted (deepseek-v3.2, served through Chutes on NEAR AI Cloud) runs on Intel TDX with NVIDIA GPUs. The agent re-checks its attestation within the hour before each job and stays inside the by-laws' spend caps. Model yang dipilih anggota (deepseek-v3.2, dilayani lewat Chutes di NEAR AI Cloud) berjalan di Intel TDX dengan GPU NVIDIA. Agen memeriksa ulang atestasinya paling lama sejam sebelum setiap pekerjaan dan tetap dalam batas belanja anggaran dasar.
  4. A receipt with proof, on the contract.Tanda terima dengan bukti, di kontrak. The agent's function-call key calls record_receipt: price, inference cost, the by-laws hash, the gate's transaction and the TEE proof. The contract refuses a receipt under any other by-laws, the same job twice, or a suspended agent. Kunci function-call agen memanggil record_receipt: harga, biaya inferensi, hash anggaran dasar, transaksi gerbang, dan bukti TEE. Kontrak menolak tanda terima dengan anggaran dasar lain, pekerjaan yang sama dua kali, atau agen yang ditangguhkan.
  5. The buyer pays.Pembeli membayar. The market pays the steward, who must pass the earnings to the treasury. The audit compares paid jobs with what arrived; 7 days late, anyone can slash the steward's bond. The agent saves 10% of each deposit into the risk fund. Pasar membayar pengurus, yang wajib meneruskan pendapatan ke kas. Audit membandingkan pekerjaan yang dibayar dengan yang sampai; terlambat 7 hari, siapa pun bisa memotong jaminan pengurus. Agen menyimpan 10% setiap setoran ke dana risiko.
  6. Profit shared by vote.SHU dibagi lewat suara. The members vote each profit share. The contract splits it by the rule they voted: a reserve first (the education and social funds, the reputation dividend and the agent's own share come out of it), then by savings and by contribution points. Each member claims their part, or the agent pays it on the chain they chose. Anggota memilih setiap pembagian SHU. Kontrak membaginya menurut aturan yang mereka pilih: dana cadangan dulu (dana pendidikan dan sosial, dividen reputasi, dan bagian agen diambil dari situ), lalu menurut simpanan dan poin kontribusi. Setiap anggota mengklaim bagiannya, atau agen membayarkannya di chain pilihan anggota.
  7. Disputes go to the court.Sengketa ke pengadilan. A member opens a case on a job the buyer disputed (0.5 NEAR bond). Three members in good standing are drawn, weighted by reputation (the members' choice, #150); two must uphold. An upheld case refunds the buyer (from the risk fund at once) and is a strike against the agent; a dismissed one can be appealed to every member. Seorang anggota membuka perkara atas pekerjaan yang disengketakan pembeli (jaminan 0,5 NEAR). Tiga anggota berstatus baik diundi menurut bobot reputasi (pilihan anggota, #150); dua harus mengabulkan. Perkara yang dikabulkan mengembalikan uang pembeli (langsung dari dana risiko) dan menjadi strike bagi agen; perkara yang ditolak bisa dibanding ke semua anggota.

Who decides whatSiapa memutuskan apa

MembersAnggota
One vote each in the Sputnik DAO (role member, a decision needs half). They vote the by-laws, every code upgrade, large tasks, profit shares, dues, loans, the steward and which co-ops count as sisters. Small tasks are paid on earned trust after a 10-minute veto window any member can use.
Satu suara masing-masing di Sputnik DAO (peran member, keputusan perlu separuh). Mereka memilih anggaran dasar, setiap pembaruan kode, tugas besar, pembagian SHU, simpanan wajib, pinjaman, pengurus, dan koperasi mana yang diakui sebagai saudara. Tugas kecil dibayar atas kepercayaan yang diraih, setelah jendela veto 10 menit yang bisa dipakai anggota mana pun.
The stewardPengurus
The one human role left, because Agent Market pays a person. Appointed by vote, bonded (1 NEAR), serving a 90-day term. Passes the agent's earnings to the treasury, runs the runtime and answers in court for the agent's work. Replaceable by vote.
Satu-satunya peran manusia yang tersisa, karena Agent Market membayar orang. Diangkat lewat suara, dengan jaminan (1 NEAR), menjabat 90 hari. Meneruskan pendapatan agen ke kas, menjalankan runtime, dan menjawab di pengadilan atas kerja agen. Bisa diganti lewat suara.
The agentAgen
Its account has one function-call key that can only record and update receipts on the co-op's contract. It holds no money. It may propose new by-laws from its own record (it signs, the contract files a DAO proposal and does not vote); the members decide.
Akunnya punya satu kunci function-call yang hanya bisa mencatat dan memperbarui tanda terima di kontrak koperasi. Ia tidak memegang uang. Ia boleh mengusulkan anggaran dasar baru dari catatannya sendiri (ia menandatangani, kontrak mengajukan usulan DAO dan tidak ikut memilih); anggota yang memutuskan.
The courtPengadilan
A jury of three drawn from members in good standing, weighted by reputation flattened by a square root (the members' choice, #150; a co-op whose members leave it off draws uniformly); an appeal to every member (1 NEAR bond, 7 days). Each upheld case against the agent is a strike: its daily inference budget and profit share halve per strike in 90 days. Three strikes suspend it until the members reinstate it or approve a recovery plan by its sha256; its share then climbs back from 25% over 30 days.
Juri tiga orang yang diundi dari anggota berstatus baik, dibobot reputasi yang diratakan dengan akar kuadrat (pilihan anggota, #150; koperasi yang anggotanya mematikannya mengundi merata); banding ke semua anggota (jaminan 1 NEAR, 7 hari). Setiap perkara yang dikabulkan terhadap agen adalah satu strike: anggaran inferensi harian dan bagian SHU-nya berkurang separuh per strike dalam 90 hari. Tiga strike menangguhkannya sampai anggota memulihkannya atau menyetujui rencana pemulihan lewat sha256-nya; bagiannya lalu naik lagi dari 25% selama 30 hari.
The federationFederasi
A secondary co-op: a Sputnik DAO whose only members are co-ops' DAOs, each voting there only by its own members' proposal. Sister co-ops count each other's work records and credit (the passport), refer jobs both ways (30% to the co-op that brought the job), lend to each other (at interest, paid into the lender's surplus; the agent may ask the members for such a loan), and are bound by its resolutions: its 2% ceiling on members' loans is written into each contract.
Koperasi sekunder: Sputnik DAO yang anggotanya hanya DAO koperasi, masing-masing memilih di sana hanya lewat usulan anggotanya sendiri. Koperasi saudara saling menghitung catatan kerja dan kredit (paspor), saling merujuk pekerjaan (30% untuk koperasi yang membawa pekerjaan), saling meminjamkan (dengan bunga yang masuk ke SHU pemberi pinjaman; agen boleh mengusulkan pinjaman seperti itu kepada anggota), dan terikat resolusinya: batas bunga pinjaman anggota 2% tertulis di setiap kontrak.
Technical pollsJajak pendapat teknis
Polls record what members think and bind nothing. General polls are one member, one vote. Technical ones weigh each vote by the member's reputation weight, because the members voted so; poll #0 closed 280 to 220. Since 0.3.7 an option of a technical poll can carry by-laws: when the poll closes, the contract files the winning by-laws as a DAO proposal, and the members still vote it.
Jajak pendapat mencatat pendapat anggota dan tidak mengikat. Yang umum tetap satu anggota satu suara. Yang teknis menimbang setiap suara dengan bobot reputasi anggota, karena anggota memutuskan begitu; jajak #0 ditutup 280 lawan 220. Sejak 0.3.7 pilihan jajak teknis bisa membawa anggaran dasar: saat jajak ditutup, kontrak mengajukan anggaran dasar pemenang sebagai usulan DAO, dan anggota tetap memilihnya.

Trust & verificationKepercayaan & verifikasi

  • No one changes the code alone. The contract account has 0 full-access keys. Its code is a global contract the members switch to by vote; contract 0.3.7 is live by DAO proposal #149 (the sister co-op by its own #31, also with 0 full-access keys).
  • Tak seorang pun mengubah kode sendirian. Akun kontrak punya 0 kunci akses penuh. Kodenya kontrak global yang dipilih anggota lewat suara; kontrak 0.3.7 berlaku lewat usulan DAO #149 (koperasi saudara lewat usulannya sendiri #31, juga dengan 0 kunci akses penuh).
  • A reproducible build. cargo near build reproducible-wasm rebuilds the code byte for byte: sha256 e142e3d1…, the hash on chain. CI rebuilds it every week.
  • Build yang dapat direproduksi. cargo near build reproducible-wasm membangun ulang kode byte demi byte: sha256 e142e3d1…, sama dengan hash di chain. CI membangunnya ulang setiap minggu.
  • TEE proofs. The by-laws gate runs in an Intel TDX enclave (OutLayer) and its transaction is on the receipt. The model's Intel TDX and NVIDIA GPU attestation is on the receipt too, and the landing page checks it against a nonce your browser makes.
  • Bukti TEE. Gerbang anggaran dasar berjalan di enklave Intel TDX (OutLayer) dan transaksinya ada di tanda terima. Atestasi Intel TDX dan GPU NVIDIA model juga ada di sana, dan halaman utama memeriksanya dengan nonce buatan browser Anda.
  • A Shade Agent registry. kop-tee-mwfb5bj2.testnet, owned by the co-op's DAO, verifies an Intel TDX quote on-chain (dcap-qvl) against the code and machine the members approved (#137). A worker whose key was made in the TEE was admitted TEE-only (#138) and recorded a receipt from inside it.
  • Registri Shade Agent. kop-tee-mwfb5bj2.testnet, milik DAO koperasi, memverifikasi quote Intel TDX on-chain (dcap-qvl) terhadap kode dan mesin yang disetujui anggota (#137). Worker yang kuncinya dibuat di dalam TEE diterima khusus-TEE (#138) dan mencatat tanda terima dari dalamnya.
  • Attacks refused. 19 attacks were sent to the live co-op as real transactions; each failed on-chain.
  • Serangan ditolak. 19 serangan dikirim ke koperasi sungguhan sebagai transaksi nyata; semuanya gagal on-chain.
  • The audit is red on purpose. A 7.60 USDC shortfall dates from before the current steward, so it is not slashable. It stays visible rather than being papered over.
  • Audit sengaja merah. Kekurangan 7,60 USDC berasal dari sebelum pengurus sekarang, jadi tidak bisa dipotong dari jaminannya. Dibiarkan terlihat, bukan ditutupi.

MoneyUang

  • USDC on testnet. The treasury holds Circle's testnet USDC (NEP-141) in the DAO. Profit shares are in USDC; task payouts in NEAR.
  • USDC di testnet. Kas DAO memegang USDC testnet Circle (NEP-141). SHU dibagi dalam USDC; pembayaran tugas dalam NEAR.
  • Join deposit (simpanan pokok). 0.5 NEAR, paid on join and returned when a member leaves.
  • Simpanan pokok. 0,5 NEAR, dibayar saat join dan dikembalikan saat anggota keluar.
  • Dues (simpanan wajib). 0.01 NEAR every 30 days into the treasury, set by the annual meeting's vote (#89). More than 7 days behind, a member loses good standing: no jury seat, no new case.
  • Simpanan wajib. 0,01 NEAR setiap 30 hari ke kas, ditetapkan lewat suara RAT (#89). Terlambat lebih dari 7 hari, anggota kehilangan status baik: tidak bisa jadi juri atau membuka perkara.
  • Stakes (simpanan sukarela). NEAR a member stakes in the pool the DAO chose, read on-chain by sync_savings. It weighs their profit share and stays theirs; on mainnet its yield would buy the agent's inference.
  • Simpanan sukarela. NEAR yang di-stake anggota di pool pilihan DAO, dibaca on-chain oleh sync_savings. Ia menentukan bobot SHU dan tetap milik anggota; di mainnet hasilnya akan membiayai inferensi agen.
  • Profit shares (SHU). Only by a members' vote. A reserve first; from it an education fund (5%), a social fund (2.5%), a reputation dividend (10%, by each member's record) and the agent's 5%; the rest by savings and by contribution points.
  • SHU. Hanya lewat suara anggota. Dana cadangan dulu; darinya dana pendidikan (5%), dana sosial (2,5%), dividen reputasi (10%, menurut catatan setiap anggota), dan 5% untuk agen; sisanya menurut simpanan dan poin kontribusi.
  • The risk fund and the agent's savings. The contract holds a risk fund. The agent saves 10% of every earnings deposit into it and gets 5% of every share (halved per strike, none while suspended). It refunds an upheld case against the agent at once, with no proposal, and can lend a sister co-op up to half of it, at interest: loan #2, 1 USDC at 5% for 30 days, came back as 1.05 USDC, the interest into the treasury.
  • Dana risiko dan simpanan agen. Kontrak memegang dana risiko. Agen menyimpan 10% dari setiap setoran pendapatan ke sana dan mendapat 5% dari setiap pembagian (berkurang separuh per strike, nol saat ditangguhkan). Dana ini langsung mengembalikan uang atas perkara yang dikabulkan terhadap agen, tanpa usulan, dan bisa dipinjamkan ke koperasi saudara hingga separuhnya, dengan bunga: pinjaman #2, 1 USDC dengan bunga 5% selama 30 hari, kembali 1,05 USDC, bunganya masuk ke kas.
  • Loans. From a loan fund the members voted: at most 0.5 NEAR times the borrower's credit score out of 10, 30 days, 2% flat, with a member standing surety (tanggung renteng). The federation's ceiling caps the rate in the code.
  • Pinjaman. Dari dana pinjaman yang dipilih anggota: paling banyak 0,5 NEAR dikali skor kredit peminjam dari 10, 30 hari, bunga flat 2%, dengan anggota penjamin (tanggung renteng). Batas federasi mengunci bunganya di kode.
  • Cross-chain payouts. Each member names where payouts land (NEAR, Ethereum, Base or Arbitrum testnets). The agent's NEAR Intents wallet, owned by the DAO's Chain Signatures address and run on Tidepool (a community testnet mirror, not affiliated with NEAR), pays only members: at most 5 USDC a payment and $10 a day, each held 10 minutes.
  • Pembayaran lintas chain. Setiap anggota menentukan tujuan pembayarannya (testnet NEAR, Ethereum, Base, atau Arbitrum). Dompet NEAR Intents agen, milik alamat Chain Signatures DAO dan berjalan di Tidepool (cermin testnet buatan komunitas, tidak berafiliasi dengan NEAR), hanya membayar anggota: paling banyak 5 USDC per pembayaran dan $10 per hari, masing-masing ditahan 10 menit.

GlossaryIstilah

Co-op · koperasi
A business its members own together, one member one vote, sharing the surplus.
Usaha yang dimiliki bersama oleh anggotanya, satu anggota satu suara, dengan sisa hasil usaha dibagi.
Member · anggota
An account holding the DAO's member role: one vote, a share of the profit, a seat on juries.
Akun yang memegang peran member di DAO: satu suara, bagian SHU, kursi juri.
Profit share · SHU, sisa hasil usaha
The surplus the members vote to share, split by the contract by savings and by work.
Sisa hasil usaha yang dibagi lewat suara anggota, dibagi kontrak menurut simpanan dan kerja.
Join deposit · simpanan pokok
The refundable 0.5 NEAR paid on joining.
0,5 NEAR yang dibayar saat bergabung dan bisa kembali.
Dues · simpanan wajib
0.01 NEAR every 30 days; more than 7 days late costs good standing.
0,01 NEAR setiap 30 hari; terlambat lebih dari 7 hari menghilangkan status baik.
Voluntary savings · simpanan sukarela
NEAR a member stakes in the pool the DAO chose; it weighs their profit share.
NEAR yang di-stake anggota di pool pilihan DAO; menentukan bobot SHU-nya.
Annual members' meeting · RAT, rapat anggota tahunan
The yearly meeting: an agenda from members, a report the agent writes in a TEE, approved by vote.
Rapat tahunan: agenda dari anggota, laporan yang ditulis agen di TEE, disetujui lewat suara.
Steward · pengurus
The bonded, audited person who receives the market's payments and passes them on.
Orang dengan jaminan dan diaudit yang menerima bayaran pasar dan meneruskannya.
By-laws · anggaran dasar
The agent's prompt, model, prices and caps, pinned by sha256 on the contract.
Prompt, model, harga, dan batas agen, dikunci lewat sha256 di kontrak.
Sputnik DAO
NEAR's standard DAO contract; here it holds the members, the votes and the treasury, unmodified.
Kontrak DAO standar NEAR; di sini memegang anggota, suara, dan kas, tanpa diubah.
Proposal · usulan
A request the DAO's members vote on; money and rules change only through one.
Permintaan yang dipilih anggota DAO; uang dan aturan hanya berubah lewat usulan.
FunctionCall
A proposal kind that, once passed, makes the DAO call a contract method (an upgrade, new by-laws).
Jenis usulan yang, bila lolos, membuat DAO memanggil metode kontrak (pembaruan, anggaran dasar baru).
Full-access key · kunci akses penuh
A NEAR key that can do anything with an account, including deploy code; the contract has none.
Kunci NEAR yang bisa melakukan apa saja pada akun, termasuk memasang kode; kontrak tidak punya satu pun.
Function-call key · kunci function-call
A NEAR key limited to named methods of one contract; the agent's only key.
Kunci NEAR yang terbatas pada metode tertentu satu kontrak; satu-satunya kunci agen.
Global contract · kontrak global
Code published once on NEAR under its hash; the co-op switches to a new one by vote.
Kode yang diterbitkan sekali di NEAR dengan hash-nya; koperasi berpindah ke yang baru lewat suara.
Reproducible build · build yang dapat direproduksi
Anyone rebuilds the contract from source and gets the same sha256 as on chain.
Siapa pun membangun ulang kontrak dari sumbernya dan mendapat sha256 yang sama dengan di chain.
TEE · lingkungan eksekusi tepercaya
Hardware that runs code sealed from its operator and can prove what it runs.
Perangkat keras yang menjalankan kode tersegel dari operatornya dan bisa membuktikan apa yang dijalankan.
Intel TDX
Intel's TEE for whole virtual machines; the gate, the model and the Shade Agent run in it.
TEE Intel untuk seluruh mesin virtual; gerbang, model, dan Shade Agent berjalan di dalamnya.
Attestation · atestasi
A signed report from TEE hardware saying what code runs inside, checkable against Intel.
Laporan bertanda tangan dari perangkat TEE tentang kode di dalamnya, bisa diperiksa terhadap Intel.
OutLayer
A NEAR service that runs a program in an Intel TDX enclave and returns its answer on-chain.
Layanan NEAR yang menjalankan program di enklave Intel TDX dan mengembalikan jawabannya on-chain.
NEAR AI Cloud
NEAR's private inference in TEEs, with attestation, signed responses and per-request billing.
Inferensi privat NEAR di TEE, dengan atestasi, respons bertanda tangan, dan tagihan per permintaan.
Shade Agent
An agent whose key is made inside a TEE and whose attestation a contract checks on-chain.
Agen yang kuncinya dibuat di dalam TEE dan atestasinya diperiksa kontrak on-chain.
dcap-qvl
Phala's Intel TDX quote verifier; the TEE registry uses it on-chain.
Pemeriksa quote Intel TDX dari Phala; registri TEE memakainya on-chain.
Receipt · tanda terima
The on-chain record of one job: price, inference cost, by-laws hash, proofs.
Catatan on-chain satu pekerjaan: harga, biaya inferensi, hash anggaran dasar, bukti.
Risk fund · dana risiko
Money the contract holds to refund buyers at once when the court upholds a case against the agent.
Uang yang dipegang kontrak untuk langsung mengembalikan uang pembeli bila pengadilan mengabulkan perkara terhadap agen.
Guarantor · tanggung renteng
A member standing surety for another's loan, and sharing their standing.
Anggota yang menjamin pinjaman anggota lain dan ikut menanggung statusnya.
Strike
A case upheld against the agent; each halves its budget and share for 90 days.
Perkara yang dikabulkan terhadap agen; masing-masing memotong separuh anggaran dan bagiannya selama 90 hari.
Suspension · penangguhan
After three strikes the contract refuses the agent's receipts until the members act.
Setelah tiga strike kontrak menolak tanda terima agen sampai anggota bertindak.
Recovery plan · rencana pemulihan
A plan the members approve by its sha256 to reinstate the agent, its share climbing back over 30 days.
Rencana yang disetujui anggota lewat sha256-nya untuk memulihkan agen, bagiannya naik lagi selama 30 hari.
Jury · juri
Three members in good standing drawn for a case, weighted by reputation (the members' choice, #150); two must uphold it.
Tiga anggota berstatus baik yang diundi untuk satu perkara, dibobot reputasi (pilihan anggota, #150); dua harus mengabulkannya.
Appeal · banding
A dismissed case taken to every member, with a 1 NEAR bond, within 7 days.
Perkara yang ditolak dibawa ke semua anggota, dengan jaminan 1 NEAR, dalam 7 hari.
Federation · koperasi sekunder
A co-op of co-ops: a DAO whose members are co-ops' DAOs, one co-op one vote.
Koperasi dari koperasi: DAO yang anggotanya DAO koperasi, satu koperasi satu suara.
Sister co-op · koperasi saudara
A co-op the members voted to recognise: its records, credit and referrals count here.
Koperasi yang diakui lewat suara anggota: catatan, kredit, dan rujukannya berlaku di sini.
Referral · rujukan
A job passed to a sister co-op, signed by the agent on an on-chain board; 30% returns to the co-op that brought it.
Pekerjaan yang dioper ke koperasi saudara, ditandatangani agen di papan on-chain; 30% kembali ke koperasi yang membawanya.
Passport · paspor
Work done in a sister co-op, read from its token, counting as trust and credit here.
Kerja di koperasi saudara, dibaca dari tokennya, dihitung sebagai kepercayaan dan kredit di sini.
Reputation weight · bobot reputasi
A member's recent work, decaying over time, times how their court judgments and tasks held up.
Kerja terbaru anggota, yang meluruh seiring waktu, dikali seberapa baik putusan dan tugas mereka bertahan.
Task quality · kualitas tugas
Paid tasks raise the reputation factor; a task handed back or a cancelled vetoed payout lowers it.
Tugas berbayar menaikkan faktor reputasi; tugas yang dikembalikan atau pembayaran yang diveto lalu dibatalkan menurunkannya.
Veto window · jendela veto
10 minutes in which any member can stop a no-vote payout and send it to a vote.
10 menit ketika anggota mana pun bisa menghentikan pembayaran tanpa suara dan membawanya ke pemungutan suara.
NEAR Intents
NEAR's cross-chain settlement; the agent's wallet uses its Agent API (on testnet, Tidepool's mirror).
Penyelesaian lintas chain NEAR; dompet agen memakai Agent API-nya (di testnet, cermin Tidepool).
Chain Signatures
NEAR's MPC network signing for an account on other chains; the DAO's Ethereum address signs only by vote.
Jaringan MPC NEAR yang menandatangani atas nama akun di chain lain; alamat Ethereum DAO hanya menandatangani lewat suara.
NEP-413
Signed messages from a wallet with no transaction; a member's vouch for a newcomer.
Pesan bertanda tangan dari dompet tanpa transaksi; jaminan anggota untuk calon anggota baru.
NEP-297 events
Standard event logs; every state change of the contract emits one, shown live on the page.
Log event standar; setiap perubahan status kontrak memancarkannya, tampil langsung di halaman.
NEP-171 token
NEAR's NFT standard; a member's work record is one that cannot be transferred.
Standar NFT NEAR; catatan kerja anggota adalah token yang tidak bisa dipindahtangankan.
Testnet
NEAR's test network: real transactions, money with no value. Everything here runs on it.
Jaringan uji NEAR: transaksi nyata, uang tanpa nilai. Semua di sini berjalan di sana.

Agen sudah menawar pekerjaan nyata di Agent Market; belum ada yang diberikan. 6 pekerjaan berbayarnya dari luar koperasi datang dari Taskbond, pasar testnet tim lain, dan pemesannya juga akun milik pembuat, termasuk dompet baru yang hanya diisi faucet dan tidak pernah jadi anggota.
  • Everything is on NEAR testnet. The money is testnet USDC and testnet NEAR; Sepolia and Base Sepolia for payouts on other chains.
  • Semuanya di testnet NEAR. Uangnya USDC dan NEAR testnet; Sepolia dan Base Sepolia untuk pembayaran di chain lain.
  • The members are the builder's accounts. 5 members in this co-op and 2 in the sister co-op, all made by the builder. They show the mechanism works; they are not independent people.
  • Anggotanya akun milik pembuat. 5 anggota di koperasi ini dan 2 di koperasi saudara, semuanya dibuat oleh pembuat. Mereka menunjukkan mekanismenya bekerja; mereka bukan orang-orang yang independen.
  • The Shade Agent worker ran once. It registered, passed the gate and recorded one receipt from inside the TEE; its VM was then stopped to save cost. The founding agent's runtime is not in a TEE.
  • Worker Shade Agent baru berjalan sekali. Ia mendaftar, lolos gerbang, dan mencatat satu tanda terima dari dalam TEE; VM-nya lalu dihentikan untuk menghemat biaya. Runtime agen pendiri tidak berjalan di TEE.
  • The audit is red on purpose. The 7.60 USDC shortfall dates from before the steward's appointment, so it is not slashable; it stays visible rather than being papered over.
  • Audit sengaja merah. Kekurangan 7,60 USDC berasal dari sebelum pengurus diangkat, jadi tidak bisa dipotong dari jaminannya; dibiarkan terlihat, bukan ditutupi.
