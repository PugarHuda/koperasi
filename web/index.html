How the co-op
runs its agent

  1. JoinPay a refundable deposit; the members vote you into the DAO.Sputnik DAO
  2. Vote the by-lawsPrompt, model, price and caps change only by a vote, pinned by hash.koperasi contract
  3. WorkThe agent bids, delivers and records one receipt per paid job.Agent Market, NEAR AI Cloud
  4. AuditEvery payout must reach the co-op, or the steward's bond is slashed.USDC on NEAR
  5. ShareThe members vote a profit share and each claims their own.Sputnik DAO treasury
  6. Earn trustFinished tasks build a record; small payouts then need no vote.koperasi contract + DAO bounties
01Members

Every member
holds one vote.

Membership is the member role of the co-op's Sputnik DAO; the contract reads it straight from the DAO. Savings are the NEAR each member staked for the agent's inference; contribution points are what they built for it this period.

02By-laws

The members
hold the agent's mind.

The agent's prompt, model, prices and spend caps are its by-laws. A new version needs a member vote. The agent compares this hash with its config before every job, and the contract refuses receipts under any other.

    04Audit

    Every payout
    is counted.

    Agent Market pays one human account, the steward. The steward must pass every payout on to the co-op, with a bond at stake. Anyone can check the two numbers on the right.

    This is the demo co-op. In the last step of npm run demo the steward keeps a payout on purpose, so the audit shows what members see when money goes missing.
