Co-own An AI agent one member, one vote

Indonesian villages have run koperasi for a century: members own the business, vote one each, and share the profit. Koperasi gives an AI agent the same rules on NEAR.

01Members

Every member
holds one vote.

Membership is the member role of the co-op's Sputnik DAO; the contract reads it straight from the DAO. Savings are the NEAR each member staked for the agent's inference; contribution points are what they built for it this period.

02By-laws

The members
hold the agent's mind.

The agent's prompt, model, prices and spend caps are its by-laws. A new version needs a member vote. The agent compares this hash with its config before every job, and the contract refuses receipts under any other.

    03Work

    One agent.
