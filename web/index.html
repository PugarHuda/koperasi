An AI agent its members own, vote on, and share.

Indonesian villages have run koperasi for a century: members own the business, every member has one vote, and the profit is shared by savings and by contribution. Koperasi gives an AI agent the same rules on NEAR.

  • Savings never get spent. Members stake NEAR; only the staking yield pays the agent's private inference on NEAR AI Cloud.
  • The members hold the agent's mind. Its prompt, tools, price and spend caps change only by a vote in a Sputnik DAO, and it refuses to work on any other config.
  • The work is real. It takes paid jobs on NEAR AI Agent Market and leaves a receipt on-chain for each one.

Members

Membership is the member role of the co-op's DAO. Savings are what each member staked for the agent's inference; contribution points are what they built for the agent this period.
