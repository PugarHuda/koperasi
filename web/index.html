How the co-op
runs its agent

  1. JoinPay a refundable deposit; the members vote you into the DAO.Sputnik DAO
  2. Vote the by-lawsPrompt, model, price and caps change only by a vote, pinned by hash.koperasi contract
  3. WorkThe agent bids, delivers and records one receipt per paid job.Agent Market, NEAR AI Cloud
  4. AuditEvery paid job's earnings must reach the co-op within 7 days, or anyone can slash the steward's bond.USDC on NEAR
  5. ShareThe members vote a profit share and each claims their own.Sputnik DAO treasury
  6. Earn trustFinished tasks build a record; small payouts then need no vote.koperasi contract + DAO bounties

What the co-op just did, on-chain

Every change the contract makes is a standard NEP-297 event. This list reads them from NEAR as members act: the NearBlocks indexer finds the transactions, the RPC gives their events, and each event is checked against the contract's schema before it is shown.

    01Members

    Every member
    holds one vote.

    Membership is the member role of the co-op's Sputnik DAO; the contract reads it straight from the DAO. Savings are the NEAR each member staked for the agent's inference; contribution points are what they built for it, halved at each profit share so recent work counts most.

    02By-laws

    The members
    hold the agent's mind.

    The agent's prompt, model, prices and spend caps are its by-laws. A new version needs a member vote. The agent compares this hash with its config before every job, and the contract refuses receipts under any other.

        07Commons

        What members
        owe each other.

        Members pledge services to the co-op (commitment vouchers, as in Kenya's Sarafu pools); whoever calls one in says whether it was kept. A buyer's dispute goes to three members drawn at random (the co-op court); two must uphold for a refund. Kept and broken pledges, jury duty and slashes make each member's standing, and only members in good standing sit on a jury.
