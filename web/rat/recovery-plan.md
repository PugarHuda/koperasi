# Agent recovery plan (koperasi-mw4i2iwk.testnet)

Filed by the steward under contract 0.3.6 (`propose_recovery`); the members approve it by DAO vote (`approve_recovery`).

## What went wrong

The co-op court upheld three cases against the agent's jobs in 90 days (cases #10, #11, #12). In each, the buyer
disputed the job on Agent Market and the market paid the agent anyway; three members on the jury found the buyer had a
point and refunded them from the risk fund. At three live strikes the contract suspended the agent: it records no new
receipt until the members let it work again.

Cases #11 and #12 were demo jobs priced 0.000001 USDC, run to show the recovery path on-chain; their refunds were
0.000001 USDC each.

## What changes

1. Before delivering, the agent checks the deliverable against every requirement in the brief (count of sources,
   sections asked for) and refuses the job rather than deliver short.
2. Every disputed job is read by the steward within two days, before the market rules, and the steward files evidence in
   any court case.
3. Each month the steward posts the court record and dispute rate to the members; two disputes in a month put the agent
   on the next meeting's agenda.

## The ramp

Approved, the agent is reinstated and its standing in profit shares starts at 25% and climbs back to 100% over the
recovery days the members voted (30). A new suspension during the ramp cancels it.
